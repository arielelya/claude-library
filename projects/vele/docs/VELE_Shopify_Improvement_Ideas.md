# VELE — Shopify Improvement Ideas (summary)
Written Nov 8, 2026. Competitors reviewed: Lululemon, Alo Yoga, Vuori, Sporty & Rich, Skims. Ideas below are PROPOSALS, not decisions. Some issues listed in the original were fixed after Nov 8 (see CLAUDE.md backlog for the current state).

## A. Issues on our site (from the Nov 8 review)
High:
1. Product page lacked size selector, description/fabric/fit, size guide link and trust elements. (Size selector: caused by missing size variants on the checked product, not a theme bug. Essential Bra now has size variants.)
2. Footer social icons linked to YouTube. (Fixed Nov 17.)
3. Product gallery: one large cropped image, no thumbnails. Needs 4–6 clean photos (front, back, detail, flat lay). Deferred until photography.
4. SEO: homepage title is just "VELE", no meta description, og/twitter empty.
5. Button hierarchy: "Add to Wishlist" heavier than "Add to cart". Make Add-to-cart a solid charcoal button, wishlist a small heart.
Medium:
6. Email popup fired mid-scroll. (Now exit-intent on desktop, delayed on mobile.)
7. Duplicates: tagline repeated; three email-capture points (block, popup, footer).
8. Shop All: 5-column grid with 7 products leaves an uneven second row; no quick-add; filters only availability and price.
9. "Made For Movement" banner is a baked image with text clashing with Cardo; "Shop the Look" hotspots look unfinished.
10. Empty cart lacks a free-shipping bar (threshold ₪250) and suggestions.
11. Mobile: header icons about 16px and tiny menu close button; raise to 44px tap targets.
12. No About page link (About page now exists as "The VELE Way").
Low:
13. Quiet Ritual section image does not match the palette; icon styles inconsistent.
14. Low contrast in the announcement bar; two different dark greens/charcoals.
15. "You may also like" loads late with grey boxes; sticky add-to-cart is grey with low contrast.
16. Inconsistent labels ("Choose"/"Add"), missing image alt text, price shown as ₪120.00 (prefer ₪120).

## B. Ideas from competitors
Small effort:
- Product page structure (Alo, Lululemon): badge, rating, price, colors, one fit line ("True to size"), size guide, solid black "Add to bag", shipping/returns line right under it, caption "Model is 5'10" wearing XS".
- Dark sticky add-to-cart on scroll.
- Calm hero (Alo): one image, headline bottom-left, one button.
- Text-only menu of 4–5 columns. Horizontal filter pills.
- Large story paragraph under the button, Details / Materials & Care as accordions.
- Single announcement line, no discount in the popup, Fabric & Care page, "Edit" collections.
- Spacing and motion: big padding, slow fade, second image on hover.
Medium effort:
- Editorial tile in the grid after row 1. Collection intro explaining fabrics. Quick-add with size strip on hover.
- "Pairs great with" in add-to-cart, free gift above a threshold.
- Reviews with fit scale (Judge.me / Okendo / Loox). Drop waiting page with date. "New to VELE?" page. Journal.
- Bundles with tiered discount, free-shipping bar.
Later: loyalty program, resale, app, RTL checks for Bit / Apple Pay / VAT in checkout.

## C. What not to copy
Hero carousels, dense navigation, chip rows, loud discounts and Final Sale; aggressive popups and spec-heavy product pages; expensive personalization stacks; unconditional free shipping/returns.

## D. Already working
Wordmark and wide typography on cream, Cardo headings, warm consistent photography, clean product cards, search, mobile menu, dark signup block, real ₪ prices, help pages and FAQ.

## E. Deferred on purpose until real products / photography exist
- Product descriptions (fabric, fit, care): until specs are final.
- Product galleries (4–6 images): until photography (same trigger as the payment provider).
- Reviews module: after first real orders. Choosing the app is fine, installing is not.
- Size selector: needs real variants on products.
